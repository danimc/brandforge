# Veskil visual review

Inspected the example contact sheet (preview.png in the source project) rendered with librsvg 2.62.1 and Cairo 1.18.4. The diagonal gap stayed open at mark widths of 24 and 64 px. The custom lettering was readable at lockup widths of 120 and 245 px. Primary and black were checked on Warm Paper. White was checked on Pine Ink. The previews showed no visible clipping.

The logo geometry and outline lettering were authored for this demonstration. No fonts or third-party artwork are distributed. Actual print stock, ink spread, clearance, font coverage across languages and product accessibility remain outside this fictional review.
