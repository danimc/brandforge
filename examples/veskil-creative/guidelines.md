# Veskil · Transect identity

**Promise.** Make a clear record at every stop. Speak directly and describe one action at a time. Use notebook language such as “station,” “index” and “record.”

**Artwork.** The two contours trace a survey corridor. Keep the channel open. Use the horizontal lockup on wide catalog tiles, the stacked version on centered cards and the mark alone in compact placements with a nearby brand name. Leave clear space equal to one eighth of the artboard’s shorter edge. Starting minimums are 24 px for the mark, 128 px for the horizontal lockup and 64 px for the stacked lockup. Avoid filling the gap, adding an outline, rotating or stretching the artwork.

**Color.** Use Pine Ink and Spruce on Warm Paper. Use solid reverse white on Pine Ink. Rust is for emphasis on Warm Paper. Keep functional information legible without color.

**Type.** The Veskil wordmark is custom outline lettering. Use Georgia Bold for editorial headings and Arial Regular for instructions, with serif and sans-serif fallbacks. Begin at 30/36 px and 16/24 px respectively. Verify script coverage before adding languages.

**Applications.** The digital tile leads with “Find the last stop.” The field card orders the steps as station, conditions and observation. See the application sheet for placement examples.
