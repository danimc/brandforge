# Veskil print handoff

Use solid black vectors for the cover stamp and field card. Start proofing the mark at 8 mm wide, horizontal lockup at 35 mm and stacked lockup at 18 mm. Reserve an eighth of the shorter artboard edge as clear space. Inspect ink spread at the left entrance and central bend of the contour channel on the actual stock.

Use the named sRGB colors in tokens.json as digital source references. Ask the printer for the stock, ICC output intent, separations and physical proof. Print dimensions and color conversion remain provisional until the printer signs off.
