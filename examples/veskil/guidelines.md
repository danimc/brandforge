# Veskil identity guidelines

Veskil is a fictional field notebook for volunteer habitat surveys. Write in short, specific observations. Lead with what someone should record, then explain where it goes.

Use the horizontal lockup on notebook covers and instruction cards. Use the mark alone in compact placements where the Veskil name is already visible. Start with minimum widths of 24 px for the mark and 120 px for the lockup. Reserve clear space outside the artboard of at least one eighth of its height. Preserve the diagonal gap. Avoid rotation, shadows and stretching.

Use Pine Ink for text on Warm Paper and Rust for headings or emphasis. Keep Spruce in the symbol and visual accents. Use black for single-ink applications and white over dark Pine Ink. Use Georgia Bold for headings and Arial Regular for instructions, with a serif and sans-serif fallback respectively. Start with a 30/36 px heading and 16/24 px body.

For a field instruction card, place the lockup at top left, then use the heading “Record the next stop” and an ordered list. Use angled rules sparingly to echo the folio seam. Label sections in text even when color distinguishes them.
