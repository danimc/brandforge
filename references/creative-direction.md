# Creative direction and originality

Create two or three materially distinct directions from the same strategy. Compare symbol logic, palette roles, type personality, applications, risks and tradeoffs. Include a small-scale and one-color view. Persist one JSON record per direction with a stable ID and provenance for source inputs.

Use category research to identify crowded motifs and visual whitespace. For real launches, document the comparison set, relevant markets and classes, search methods, dates and unresolved similarities. Research names and silhouettes separately. Avoid copying, tracing, prompting for a recognizable mark, or treating generated output as inherently original. Escalate material similarities for redesign and route clearance decisions to qualified review.
