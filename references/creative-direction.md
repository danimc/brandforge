# Concept Exploration Engine

Translate audience, promise, voice and use constraints into visual questions. For a field brand, ask which forms communicate recording, finding or handover under poor light. When research tools are available, inspect the category, adjacent fields, signage and references. Record source, observation and rights. Study principles such as contrast, cadence or negative space. Keep recognizable third-party artwork out of the master and concept prompt. Document geographic or category research limits. Treat a similarity review and trademark clearance as distinct activities.

Search the [reference library](reference-library.md) when a catalog is available. Carry relevant observations and provenance into the creative state.

Create 8–12 hypotheses in `creative.json`. Give each a distinct idea, metaphor, composition and personality. Consider monogram, abstract, negative space, wordmark, organic and other approaches that fit the brief. Sketch them with different visual mechanisms, such as enclosure, recurrence, typographic gesture or cutout. Variants that differ in color, rotation or treatment count as one hypothesis.

Shortlist 3–6 candidates. Compare each against the priority application, silhouette, monochrome readability, brand fit, category resemblance and open risks. Write concrete reasons for advances and eliminations. Use the shortest truthful description of what was researched. Preserve a hypothesis ID even if a later round changes its composition.

Set the mode based on the work. Choose vector-first for direct control of the silhouette and one-ink reproduction. Use image-assisted for mood and texture exploration that informs hand-built concepts. Use hybrid when an image explores a promising structural idea that will be independently reconstructed as editable vector geometry. Record the rationale and actual inputs. If a provider is unavailable, continue vector-first and record the constraint.

Build and inspect a comparison for the shortlisted concepts. Present the tradeoffs and stable IDs alongside the sheet, then stop for selection. Record the approval using the exact comparison packet and [delivery contract](deliverables.md). A request to refine a chosen direction can reuse its ID and approval. A materially different direction needs a new approval.
