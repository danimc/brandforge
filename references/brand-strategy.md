# Strategy and intake

Capture the audience’s decision, the competing alternatives, the promise and evidence behind it, voice, applications, and constraints in the brief. Write a positioning statement with the category, audience, benefit, and reason to believe. Distinguish supplied facts, research, and assumptions. Ask only for gaps that materially change the work, such as geography, language, audience, launch channels, legal ownership, or required formats.

Map each priority application to a design constraint. A tiny app icon needs a recognizable silhouette. A supplier’s single-ink process needs real knockouts. Carry those constraints into concept comparisons and guidelines.
