# Veskil before and after

Both runs use the same byte-for-byte brief and palette. Veskil is a fictional field notebook for volunteer habitat surveys. The baseline remains in [examples/veskil](../examples/veskil/) with its [released kit](../examples/veskil-kit/). The new project is in [examples/veskil-creative](../examples/veskil-creative/). Its approvals are simulated. The comparison was opened as a PNG, then the first and second Transect proofs and the application sheet were inspected at native render dimensions.

| Dimension | Baseline | Creative Engine 0.2 | Evidence type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diversity | Two documented concepts. Field Notch uses a split folio and Index Column uses tabs. | Ten documented hypotheses with six reasons for elimination. Four rendered directions use organic contours, a folded V, an observation frame and a type-led mark. | Counts and IDs are automatic. Diversity of visual mechanisms was judged by inspecting the sheet. |
| Originality | The approved diagonal split conveys a folio with few shapes. | The selected corridor follows an organic, open contour instead of the straight split. Folded V and Sample Window showed risks of looking like familiar check or focus symbols. Transect can still read as a wave outside the notebook context. | Visual judgment. No market-wide or trademark clearance assessment. |
| Vector quality | Two editable masters and six kit logo SVGs. | Three editable masters and nine kit logo SVGs. Cubic paths form both the contour and parts of the outline lettering. | Static profile, content, variant counts and hashes are automatic. Optical balance is visual. |
| Typography | Angular outline lettering stayed legible at the tested 120/245 px lockup widths. | The redrawn wordmark softens the S and works in a separate stacked arrangement. Horizontal lettering was clearer at 128 px than at 64 px. Stacked typography was legible at 64 px in the proof. | Visual inspection. No external wordmark font, reading-speed study or language coverage test. |
| Scalability | Contact sheet tested the mark at 24/64 px and horizontal at 120/245 px on approved grounds. | Three master proofs test 16/32/64/128 px on light, dark and single-ink surfaces. The revised mark opens the left corridor. The dark primary loses one band, so the guidelines specify white reverse on dark. | Sizes and file integrity are automatic. Silhouette, antialiasing and text legibility are visual. |
| Coherence | The split folio communicates a physical notebook. | The route metaphor emphasizes field movement and handover. The cover-stamp constraint is addressed with a real open gap and ink variant. | Editorial review of strategy and visual evidence. Actual stock and field usability need testing. |
| Presentation | Compact comparison of colorways and two widths for the selected mark. | A concept atlas presents four directions on consistent grounds. The application study shows a digital tile and a print field card. | Both rendered outputs were viewed. The application study is a layout, with no physical or photographic proof. |
| Reproducibility | Verified review and release manifest `bc14b4f…`. | Verified review and release manifest `f451d03…`. Includes the selected board, prior critique, revised proofs and applications. | Both manifest checks are automatic. Pixel regeneration depends on the renderer and fonts. |

The new run improves the breadth of exploration, the visibility of design tradeoffs, and the traceability of refinement. The baseline still has a direct notebook metaphor. This single fictional comparison does not establish a general preference or a commercial quality ranking.
